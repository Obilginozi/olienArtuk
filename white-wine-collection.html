The table

How to pour it.

Built for the sea — including white fish and scallop carpaccio. Tuna, salmon, and raw beef belong to Carpaccio.

White fish & scallop

Raw or cooked white fish, scallop, and shrimp. Fine almond, light spice.

Cooked seafood

Shellfish and fuller whites. A slightly firmer pour for structure.

Sparkling

Soft, round fruit so bitterness never spoils the first sip.

For white fish, scallop, shellfish, and a pale glass of white.

In the kitchen

A house oil for the sea — and a careful open bottle.

Delicate aromas are the most fragile. Prefer a smaller bottle, keep it dark and cool, and finish the plate rather than heating the oil hard.
